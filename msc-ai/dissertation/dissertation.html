My Project

Working title: Expert Human Tacit Knowledge vs. LLM Performance in Detecting AI-Generated Imitations of Classical Arabic Poetry

Teaser: Can expert human readers still beat AI at recognising what is genuine? Using fine-tuned LLM imitations of the classical Arabic poet al-Maʿarrī, this dissertation tests whether niche literary expertise yields a detection advantage over frontier language models, and whether that advantage reflects tacit, aesthetic judgement rather than explicit knowledge.
